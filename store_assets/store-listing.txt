==== FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 chars) ====

TaskManager is a clean, fully offline day planner that combines a smart to-do list, a focus timer, and reminders that work your way — everything you need to run your day from one place.

YOUR DAY, ORGANIZED
• The Today screen shows exactly what's on your plate: timed tasks slot into your schedule, urgent items float to the top, and finished ones tick off satisfyingly.
• Define your own day periods (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night…) — tasks automatically organize themselves into the right part of the day.
• Recurring tasks and a personal timetable keep routines like gym, study or medication coming back automatically: daily, weekly, monthly or any custom interval.
• At midnight everything rolls over by itself: finished tasks are filed into History, missed ones are recorded, and your routines get a fresh start. No duplicates, no clutter.

REMINDERS THAT WORK YOUR WAY
• Give any task a time and it pops up right on schedule.
• Choose how each task announces itself: silent, vibrate, chime, melody, a full alarm with constant vibration — or any sound file from your phone.
• Skip a task for the day or snooze it, straight from the notification.
• Reminders disappear by themselves after a duration you choose.

FOCUS SESSIONS
• Turn any task into a focus session with a live countdown timer.
• Pause, extend or complete it right from the notification.
• Optional Do Not Disturb for the length of the session — restored automatically when you're done.
• Customizable "almost done" warning and end-of-session alert, per task.
• See your total focus time by activity in Stats.

NOTES, ALWAYS AT HAND
• Jot quick notes and pin the important ones.
• Optional periodic notes reminder with your own message and interval — pinned notes ride along right inside the notification.

EVERYTHING AT A GLANCE
• A command-center notification with your current tasks: tap to complete, add tasks or notes without opening the app. Keep it pinned or make it swipeable — your choice.
• A resizable home-screen widget with three pages — today's tasks, completed, and notes — with quick add built in.
• A searchable History of every day: done, missed and skipped.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• 100% offline. No account, no ads, no tracking — the app doesn't even request internet access. Your data never leaves your device.

Light on your battery, heavy on your productivity. Install TaskManager and let your day organize itself.
